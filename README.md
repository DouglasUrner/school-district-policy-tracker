# US School District Policy Tracker

 Change history on selected US school district polices. Primarily focused on policies around gender, gender expression, and HIB (harrassment, intimitation, and bullying).

 The goal is to make it easier to track policy changes, to provide alerts when policies change, and to help folks who are trying to evaluate districts -- for example if you are looking for a home the area -- to see how the policies and the tenor of the board / community.

 The watching of districts is done by [Watcher](https://github.com/douglasurner/Watcher)

 ## Files

 * **states-and-territories.txt**: A list of the states and territories being watched. Other high level "entites," e.g., the Department of Education, could also be added here.

 * **districts.txt**: A list of the school districts in the state that are being watched. It can also include related organizations, e.g., the state superintendant, policy writing groups etc.

 * **policies.txt**: A list of the identifiers for the policies to track -- the assumption is that there is a common naming convention used in the state.
